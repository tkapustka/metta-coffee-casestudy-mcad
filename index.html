Project Overview

Project: Redesigned the website for Metta Coffee, a micro-batch roaster and community hub in Anoka, Minnesota.

Dual Navigation: Serves daily coffee drinkers and local artisans or pop-up vendors seeking venue space.

Design & Features: Built on a structured Swiss grid with editorial typography and warm tones, featuring a maker journal, event booking portals, and e-commerce.

  • Project TypeResponsive Website
  • IndustryCoffee Shop
  • LocationAnoka, MN
  • Year2026

Client Request

Metta Coffee requested a comprehensive website redesign to transform their disconnected, boilerplate storefront into a dual-path digital ecosystem serving both daily coffee drinkers and local artisans seeking venue partnerships.

We want to sit betweencoffee & local artists.
